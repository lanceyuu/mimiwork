Three fixes: connectors stay signed in, Connectors is easier to find, and long documents no longer get stuck.

- **Fixed**: Notion and Canva stay signed in when you reopen the app. Before, you had to reconnect them in every new session.
- **Connectors in Settings**: Settings now has a Connectors entry, so you no longer need the account menu to reach it.
- **Fixed**: when Mimi writes a long document, it now writes it in parts instead of trying the same oversized write again and again. Before, a long Word document could fail several times in a row, use up credits, and never get written.
