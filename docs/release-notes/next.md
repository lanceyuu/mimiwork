Mimi now checks its facts before it answers, and no longer calls work finished when it is not.

- **Facts get looked up, not recited**: when a reply names links, citations, journal policies, impact factors or "since 2023"-style claims and Mimi opened no source in that turn, it is asked once to look them up or to say plainly that they are unverified. This catches answers like "this journal accepts Registered Reports" given from memory.
- **Coding agents are no longer abandoned mid-run**: waiting on several agents as they finish one by one was mistaken for Mimi repeating itself, so it sometimes stopped waiting, did the work itself and reported it as the agents'. Waiting now counts as progress.
- **"Ready" means ready**: a file Mimi presents as finished is reopened first. If it still has placeholders such as "TBD" or "[to be specified]", Mimi fixes them or tells you what is still open.
- **Web pages and PDFs are read honestly**: Mimi can now read the text of PDFs it fetches from the web, and when a site refuses to be read it says it could not open the page instead of guessing what it says.
- **Reopened chats show only your words as yours**: notes Mimi gives itself, such as "you are repeating yourself", used to appear as if you had typed them after a chat was reopened. They now show as quiet notices.
