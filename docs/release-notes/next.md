Mail shows what you sent, and you can ask Mimi about your mail.

- **Sent**: Mail has a Sent tab next to the inbox tabs. Each row is a conversation you wrote in, named for who it went to, and it opens in the same reader as the inbox. Before, a message you sent that nobody answered could only be found through search. If your mailbox has no Sent folder that Mail can find, the tab says so.
- **Ask Mimi**: Mail has an Ask Mimi button. Type a question and the answer comes in a new chat. With a conversation open, Mimi reads that conversation. With nothing open, Mimi looks in your mailbox, for questions like "what did Ben ask me for last week?". Nothing is sent or changed. A very long conversation is handed over as its newest twenty messages.
