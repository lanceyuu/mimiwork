Mail that is ready when you open it, and a comment on an email with one click.

- **Comment with a click**: click anywhere in a paragraph of an email and a Comment button appears at that spot. Press it and the whole paragraph is the passage, with the cursor already in the note. Select a few words first to comment on just those. A second comment in a paragraph that already has a highlight now works.
- **Mail kept up to date**: while MimiWork is open the mailbox is synced every two minutes in the background, so the list is current the moment you open Mail. With sorting on, new mail is sorted then too, which uses credits.
- **Emails that open at once**: the text of the newest 100 inbox emails is downloaded ahead of time, so opening one no longer waits for the mail server. This copy stays on your computer, is erased when you disconnect the mailbox, and is forgotten after 30 days. The first sync after this update takes a little longer while it downloads them.
- **No pause before an email shows**: an email appears as soon as it has loaded.
