# Ads & banners

Sizes: 1200×628 (feed), 300×250 / 728×90 / 160×600 (display), 1080×1920 (story).

- One message per ad: a five-word outcome ("Interviews analyzed. Report delivered.") in
  ink, key word teal; product name + puppy `mascot/puppy-<pose>` as the only art.
- Feed ads may use a real product screenshot in a bordered card on tint; display sizes
  are too small for screenshots — statement + button only.
- CTA button: teal, white text, 4-6px radius at display sizes; "Try MimiWork free" /
  "Start with QualiTaTi" — verbs, not "Learn more" when space allows.
- Story format: teal top band (logo line), paper middle (statement + screenshot),
  teal bottom band (CTA). Thumb-zone: CTA in the lower third.
- Always readable at 50% zoom; if it isn't, remove an element rather than shrink text.
