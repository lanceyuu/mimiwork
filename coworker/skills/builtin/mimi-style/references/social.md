# Social posts (LinkedIn, X, WeChat cards)

Card canvas: 1200×630 (link/OG), 1080×1080 (square), 1200×675 (X inline).

- Composition: paper background, big ink statement (one idea, max 2 lines, 56-72px),
  the key word in teal, product name small in teal at top-left, ONE visual element —
  either a `tiles/` icon at ~120px or the mascot at ~180px, bottom-right.
- Announcement pattern: "New in MimiWork" small teal cap, statement, one screenshot in
  a bordered card tilted 0deg (never rotated), version number muted bottom-left.
- Dark variant for launches: teal background, white text, `dark/` icons, mascot in white.
- Copy voice: outcome first ("Drop an .xlsx, get the analysis"), no hashtags walls
  (max 3), no emojis in the card image; one in the post text is acceptable on LinkedIn.
- Never: red icons, gradients, more than one mascot, screenshots without a border.
