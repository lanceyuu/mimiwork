# Archetype Recipes — pick the right one, then follow its bar

JCR publishes four broad paper types, each with a *different* recipe and a *different* bar. Diagnose
the archetype before drafting; applying the wrong template is a common own-goal. Corpus mix: ~62%
quant-experimental, ~10% mixed/field-first, ~15% CCT/qualitative, ~13% conceptual/curation.

## A. Quant-experimental (the default JCR paper)

**Deliverable:** a mechanism-based causal claim, evidenced by a multi-study package.

**Structure:** Intro (see `front-end-and-positioning.md`) → **Conceptual development** (theory +
hypotheses) → **Studies** (each: brief intro, method, results, discussion) → **General Discussion**
(theoretical contributions, practical implications, limitations + future research).

**Conceptual development.** This is the *theory*, not a literature dump. For each hypothesis, give
the **why** — the psychological mechanism, drawn from the borrowed lens — in prose, before stating
the formal hypothesis. The figure (boxes/arrows) comes *with* verbal logic for every arrow. Lead
with the focal effect (H1), then the mediator (H2/H3), then moderators that *are* the mechanism
evidence (they switch the mediator on/off).

**The study cycle (the modal 5–9 study arc):**
1. Establish the **focal effect** (ideally with a consequential DV or a field/archival anchor).
2. **Mediation** — measure the mechanism M.
3. **Moderation-of-process** — manipulate M (or a switch on M) to show the effect moves.
4. **Boundary condition** — a theory-predicted moderator that attenuates/reverses the effect.
5. **Rule out the top alternative account** in a dedicated study; replicate with a real behavior.

See `study-architecture-and-evidence.md` for how hard each rung is now defended.

**Bar:** named mechanism + manipulation-of-mediator (not just measured mediation) + ≥1 real
behavioral DV + boundary condition + a believed assumption overturned.

## B. Mixed / field-first (the rising prestige type)

**Deliverable:** the same causal claim as (A), but anchored in real-world data — the highest-credibility
package.

**Recipe:** open the empirics with **large-scale archival/field data** establishing the phenomenon
is real and consequential (scanner/loyalty panels, platform data — Kickstarter, Rotten Tomatoes,
Google Trends, finance-app transactions, registry/field-experiment data), **then** lab experiments
to isolate the mechanism. For language/behavior work, the standardizing template is **NLP on a large
text corpus (sentiment/embeddings/LIWC) + confirmatory experiments.**

**Bar:** the field result must be more than descriptive — it sets up the causal question the
experiments answer; the two halves must speak to each other (the field effect is the experiments'
target). Report the data source, N, and identification clearly; reviewers probe confounds in field
data hard.

## C. CCT / qualitative

**Deliverable:** a **named, sequenced process model** or a **new mid-range construct** — *not* rich
description. Description alone does not publish at JCR anymore.

**Recipe:**
- **Import a macro social theory** as the lens (assemblage theory, social acceleration, ontological
  security, governmentality, liquid modernity, racial formation).
- **Extend *and correct* a canonical text** (Belk, Holt, Goffman, Giddens) — not just "add a context."
- **Data = depth:** multi-year, multi-source ethnography/netnography/interviews (5–7 yr panels);
  triangulation; hermeneutic/iterative coding. Depth substitutes for statistical power as the
  credibility signal.
- **Findings present a process** (stages/mechanisms with names) and a model figure; non-WEIRD or
  marginalized context used as *theoretical leverage*, not color.
- **Structure:** Intro → theoretical framing → context & method (rich) → findings (the process, in
  named stages) → discussion (the construct/model, contributions, how it corrects the canon).

**Bar:** a portable new construct or process model + a corrected/extended canonical theory + deep
credible data.

## D. Conceptual / framework / curation

**Deliverable:** a **reordering of the field** — not a review.

**Recipe:** import a macro theory (or a fresh organizing lens) not yet used in consumer research;
show it **resolves contradictions the incumbent paradigm cannot**; recast prior findings as special
cases; deliver a **structured research agenda** with concrete, testable propositions. Curations
(usually invited) reconcile a literature under one lens and set an agenda.

**Bar:** the lens must *do work* — generate propositions and resolve tensions a summary couldn't.
Pure synthesis without reordering does not appear in the corpus.

---

**Cross-archetype rule:** whatever the type, the paper must still pass the one-sentence bar — a
novel mechanism-based *why* that overturns a believed assumption. The archetype only changes *how*
the why is evidenced (experiments vs ethnography vs conceptual argument).
